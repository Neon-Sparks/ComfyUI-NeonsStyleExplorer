# previews/

Empty on purpose, and no longer used.

Up to 2.8.1 the Default catalog kept its preview images here — inside the
package, which is the part an update replaces. They now live with every other
catalog under `user/catalogs/default/previews/`, and anything left here is moved
there automatically the first time 2.9.0 loads.

Everything of yours is under `user/`. Back that up and you have backed up
your previews, catalogs, custom styles, edits, favourites and LoRA notes.
